DAR NAJAT

Feed someone in a loved one’s name.

Anyone can do this, alone or with a few friends, in an afternoon. A short playbook shows you how.

  1. ReadThe five-step playbook: what to bring and how to serve with dignity.
  2. GiveA meal, water, socks, or a care kit, in the name of someone you miss.
  3. Log itSo the next person knows that corner was cared for.
