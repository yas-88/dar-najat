Meals coming up

Neighbors who plan to bring food. Plans can change, so take these as likely, not promised.

    Food today

    Ask for free meals and food pantries near you.

    Call 2-1-1 Free and confidential. Local help for food, shelter, and more. Text your ZIP code to 898211 Oregon and Southwest Washington. Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm. Find free meals on a map Oregon Food Bank’s Food Finder. Free meals and groceries in Oregon and Southwest Washington.

    If 2-1-1 does not connect from your phone, call 1-866-698-6155. It is free too.

    A place to stay

    Shelter and housing help, including on very cold or very hot days.

    Housing Hotline 360-695-9677 Clark County, Washington. Monday to Friday 9am to 8pm. Weekends and holidays 11am to 5pm. Anywhere else: call 2-1-1 Ask for shelter near you.

    Someone to talk to

    Any hour, any day. You do not have to be in crisis to call.

    Call or text 9-8-8 The 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline. Free and confidential.

    In danger, or badly hurt

    For you, or for someone near you.

    Call 9-1-1 Emergency help, any time.
