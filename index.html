Why this exists

A meal given in their memory.

Dar Najat began with one mother, Najat, and is built for anyone. You run a feeding for people without a home, you do it in a loved one’s name, and you log it so the next person knows that corner was cared for.

It is built to be carried by many hands, not one. The records belong to everyone who shows up, so the work lives on long after any single person steps back.
