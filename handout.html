DAR NAJAT
Home Playbook Log Need a meal?

Pocket cards to hand to someone who needs a meal. Page one is English, page two is Spanish.

  • Print two-sided for English on one face and Spanish on the other, then cut along the dashed lines into four.
  • Offer one the way you offer food: ask first, and respect a no.

Page 1 · English

DAR NAJAT

Help near you, any time

Dial 2-1-1

Free and confidential. Local help for food, shelter, and more.

  • Text your ZIP code to 898211 Oregon and SW Washington, weekdays 9 to 5
  • Shelter, Clark County: Housing Hotline 360-695-9677
  • Someone to talk to, any hour: call or text 988
  • Work and training: WorkSource, esd.wa.gov

This card is free. So is every number on it.

Ayuda cerca de usted

Marque 2-1-1

Gratis y confidencial. Ayuda local para comida, refugio y más. Atienden en español.

  • Envíe su código postal por texto al 898211 Oregon y suroeste de Washington, lunes a viernes, 9 a 5
  • Refugio, condado de Clark: Línea de Vivienda 360-695-9677
  • Alguien con quien hablar, a cualquier hora: llame al 988 y oprima 2
  • Trabajo y capacitación: WorkSource, esd.wa.gov
